Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

PairMundo helps au pairs and host families find each other, talk, and manage a placement. This policy explains what information we collect, why, who we share it with, and the choices you have. It applies to the PairMundo app and the website at pairmundo.com.

Who we are

PairMundo is run by Ansokan Inc., a Delaware corporation in the United States ("we", "us"). We are responsible for your personal data. You can reach us at hello@pairmundo.com.

We only collect what you give us or what the app needs to work. We do not use advertising trackers, we do not show ads, and we do not sell your personal data.

Information about children: host families describe their children by age only. Please do not put children's names, photos or other details in your profile or bio.

Who can see your information

  • Other members see your profile (name, photos, country and city, profile details, rating and reviews). They never see your email address or password.
  • The people you chat with see your messages.
  • Our team can see accounts, reports and reviews to moderate the service and handle support requests.

You can hide your profile from Discover at any time in your profile settings.

Service providers

We use a small number of providers who process data for us, only to run PairMundo:

  • Render (hosting and database, servers in Frankfurt, Germany)
  • Cloudflare (domain, network and photo storage)
  • Resend (sending sign-up and password emails)
  • Apple, Google and RevenueCat (Family Pass purchases in the apps: Apple or Google takes the payment, and RevenueCat tells us the purchase is real; see RevenueCat’s privacy policy)
  • Stripe (Family Pass payments on the website, and the optional ID check: Stripe Identity collects and checks your ID document and selfie, and keeps them under its own privacy policy)
  • Expo, Apple and Google (delivering push notifications, if you allow them)

Some of these providers are based in the United States. Where data leaves the European Economic Area or the UK, we rely on the provider's Standard Contractual Clauses or an equivalent legal safeguard. We may also share information if the law requires it, or to protect someone's safety.

Legal bases (EEA and UK)

  • Contract: to provide the service you signed up for (your account, profile, matching, messages, placements).
  • Legitimate interests: to keep PairMundo safe, prevent fraud and abuse, and improve the service.
  • Consent: for push notifications, the optional ID check (which compares your selfie with your ID photo, a form of biometric data), and anything else we ask your permission for. You can withdraw consent at any time.
  • Legal obligation: where the law requires us to keep or share information.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is open. When you delete your account (Profile → Account and safety → Delete my account), we delete your profile, photos, likes, matches, messages, placements and the reviews linked to them straight away. Server logs are kept for a short time, usually no more than 30 days. Backups are overwritten within 30 days.

Your rights

You can see and change most of your information in the app. You can also ask us to give you a copy of your data, correct it, delete it, limit how we use it, or object to how we use it. Email hello@pairmundo.com and we will answer within one month. If you are unhappy with our answer, you can complain to your local data protection authority.

Security

Passwords are stored as secure hashes, connections use HTTPS, and email codes expire after 30 minutes. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password that you don't use anywhere else.

Age

PairMundo is for people aged 18 or over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone younger. If you think a child has an account, tell us and we will remove it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top. If the change is important, we will tell you in the app or by email first.
