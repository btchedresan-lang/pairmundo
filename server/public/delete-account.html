Delete your PairMundo account

PairMundo, published by Ansokan Inc.

You can delete your PairMundo account and its data at any time, in the app, on the website, or by email.

In the app

  1. Open PairMundo and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile and tap Account and safety.
  3. Tap Delete my account, enter your password and confirm.

On the website

  1. Sign in at pairmundo.com.
  2. Open Account and safety.
  3. Enter your password under Delete account and press Delete my account.

By email

If you can't sign in, email hello@pairmundo.com from the address on your account and ask us to delete it. We confirm it is your account and delete it within 30 days.

What is deleted

Straight away: your account, profile, photos, likes, matches, messages, placements and the reviews linked to them, references, certificates, Family Pass records, blocks, reports, saved profiles and push notification tokens.

Kept for a limited time:

  • Server logs, usually no more than 30 days.
  • Backups, overwritten within 30 days.
  • If you used the early family bonus, your email and the date you used it, so the bonus can't be claimed twice.

Google, Apple and Stripe keep their own records of any payment you made through them.

Your ID check documents are held by Stripe, not by us. See Stripe's privacy policy to ask Stripe to delete them.

See also our Privacy Policy.
